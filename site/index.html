Essence Extractor

Essence Extractor is a Chrome extension that turns a cluttered article page into a clean, readable version: just the title and the article text, with no ads, popups or sidebars.

How to use it

Open an article and click the E button in Chrome's toolbar. Reload the page to get the original back.

The reader view's theme, font and text size can be changed in the extension's settings.

On your phone: Firefox for Android

Chrome on Android doesn't support extensions, so for reading on a phone Essence Extractor is also available for Firefox for Android (and desktop Firefox). It works the same way, and the reader view adjusts to fit a phone screen. To use it, open an article, tap Firefox's menu, choose Extensions, then Essence Extractor.

Privacy

Essence Extractor does not collect, store or send any personal data. All page processing happens inside your browser. The only thing the extension saves is your choice of theme, font and text size, which is kept in your browser's own extension storage.
